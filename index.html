01 — About

Writing with curiosity, clarity, and purpose.

My background in journalism taught me to ask better questions, find the human story, and communicate information clearly — often under tight deadlines.

I've worked across journalism, digital content, social media, and communications, adapting stories for websites, news platforms, social channels, and other digital formats.

I enjoy work that sits between information and people: stories that make complex ideas accessible, communications that feel human, and content shaped around what an audience actually needs.

My experience in dynamic and multicultural environments has also taught me to listen carefully, collaborate across roles, and adapt quickly.
